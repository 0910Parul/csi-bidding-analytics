# 🏗️ CSI Competitor Bidding Analytics — Tableau Dashboard

**How can a construction firm win more sealed-bid auctions by reading its competitors?**
An analytics-consulting project for **Central Specialties Inc. (CSI)**, a highway-construction company operating across MN, ND, and SD. We analyzed first-price sealed-bid auction data and built an interactive **Tableau dashboard** of competitor bidding behavior — turning intuition-based bidding into data-informed strategy. ## 📌 Business Problem

CSI competes in **first-price, sealed-bid** construction auctions — *"like a game of poker,"* where you never see competitors' cards and win only by reading their past behavior. CSI's bidding was largely **intuition-based**. The goal: give them a data-driven view of the competitive landscape so they can bid smarter and win more profitable work.

---

## 🎯 Approach

- Analyzed first-price sealed-bid auction data across **195+ projects in 3 states**.
- Built an interactive **Tableau dashboard** tracking, per competitor: **bidding frequency, pricing aggression, win share, and geographic bidding patterns**.
- Ran **state-level competitor analysis** to identify where CSI is strong, where it's threatened, and which rivals to watch.

---

## 🔑 Key Findings

- **Minnesota win rate: 19%** (22 of 114 projects) — CSI's most contested market.
- **Duininck** bids **~7% more aggressively**, pressuring margins on shared bids.
- **Joe Riley Construction** is highly selective — a **41% success rate** on the jobs it chooses to bid.
- **South Dakota is CSI's strongest market** (~33% win rate) — a priority for focused bidding.
